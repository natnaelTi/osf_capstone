# Product UI quality gates

## Workflow integrity

- The primary action is obvious.
- Every visible control works or is clearly marked outside the demo scope.
- Back, cancel, retry, edit, and resume behavior is defined.
- The end-to-end demo does not depend on team narration.

## Regulatory integrity

- Evidence, extraction, interpretation, action, and professional judgment are distinguishable.
- Citations reveal useful source context.
- Source date/version/freshness is visible near consequential guidance.
- Conflicts and missing evidence remain visible.
- High-impact interpretation has a review path.
- No unsupported confidence, endorsement, or legal-certainty claim appears.

## Visual and interaction quality

- Hierarchy, alignment, spacing, typography, tokens, and icon style are consistent.
- Empty, loading, error, stale, conflict, review, and success states are intentional where relevant.
- No accidental horizontal scrolling or clipped content occurs.
- Dense data remains scannable without turning every value into a card.

## Accessibility

- Normal text contrast reaches 4.5:1.
- Focus is visible and not obscured.
- Keyboard order matches visual order.
- Controls have names, labels, states, and adequate targets.
- Validation is announced and linked to fields.
- Status is not conveyed by color alone.
- Zoom and reduced motion work.

## Verification record

Record commands run, viewports checked, keyboard/reduced-motion checks, known gaps, and whether screenshots or a live render were actually inspected.
